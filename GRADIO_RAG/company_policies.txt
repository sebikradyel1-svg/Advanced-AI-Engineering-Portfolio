                    SECTION 1: TIME OFF & LEAVE POLICIES
================================================================================

1.1 PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
-----------------------
All full-time employees are entitled to paid time off based on their tenure:
- 0-2 years of service: 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- 3-5 years of service: 20 days per year (1.67 days per month)
- 6-10 years of service: 25 days per year (2.08 days per month)
- 10+ years of service: 30 days per year (2.5 days per month)

PTO accrues on the first day of each month. Unused PTO can be carried over to 
the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any PTO beyond 10 days will be 
forfeited on December 31st unless approved by HR for special circumstances.

Part-time employees receive prorated PTO based on their scheduled hours.

1.2 HOW TO REQUEST TIME OFF
---------------------------
To request time off, employees must:
1. Submit a request through the HR Portal (hr.techcorp.com) at least 2 weeks 
   in advance for planned vacations
2. For urgent requests (less than 2 weeks), email your direct manager and CC 
   hr@techcorp.com
3. Wait for approval before making travel arrangements
4. Requests during peak business periods (Q4, product launches) may be limited

Emergency time off: In case of emergencies, notify your manager as soon as 
possible via phone or text, then submit the formal request within 24 hours.

1.3 SICK LEAVE
--------------
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave 
can be used for:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments (doctor, dentist, specialist)
- Caring for an immediate family member who is ill
- Mental health days

If you are sick for more than 3 consecutive days, you must provide a doctor's 
note upon return. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out upon 
termination.

1.4 PARENTAL LEAVE
------------------
TechCorp offers comprehensive parental leave:

Primary Caregiver (birth/adoption):
- 16 weeks fully paid leave
- Can be taken consecutively or split within the first year
- Additional 4 weeks at 60% pay if needed

Secondary Caregiver:
- 6 weeks fully paid leave
- Must be taken within 6 months of birth/adoption

Adoption & Foster Care:
- Same benefits as biological parents
- Additional 5 days paid leave for legal proceedings

To apply: Submit parental leave request to HR at least 8 weeks before expected 
leave date (or as soon as possible for adoption).

1.5 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
---------------------
Paid bereavement leave is provided as follows:
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle): 3 days
- Close friend or colleague: 1 day

Additional unpaid leave may be approved upon request.

1.6 JURY DUTY & VOTING
----------------------
Employees called for jury duty will receive full pay for up to 10 days. Provide 
your jury summons to HR. If jury duty extends beyond 10 days, contact HR to 
discuss options.

For voting, employees may take up to 2 hours paid time off on election days if 
their work schedule does not allow sufficient time to vote.

1.7 HOLIDAYS
------------
TechCorp observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday of January)
- Presidents' Day (Third Monday of February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday of May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday of September)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday of November)
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (December 24)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- New Year's Eve (December 31)

Floating Holidays: Employees also receive 2 floating holidays per year to use 
for religious observances, cultural celebrations, or personal occasions.

                 SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK & FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES
================================================================================

2.1 REMOTE WORK POLICY
----------------------
TechCorp supports a hybrid work model. Eligible employees may work remotely 
up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval and role requirements.

Eligibility Requirements:
- Completed probationary period (90 days)
- Demonstrated ability to work independently
- Role does not require physical presence
- Satisfactory performance reviews

Remote Work Guidelines:
- Maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace
- Ensure reliable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps)
- Be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Attend all required in-person meetings and events
- Use company-approved VPN for all work activities
- Keep your calendar updated with your location

2.2 FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES
----------------------
Employees may request flexible schedules, including:
- Compressed work week (4 x 10-hour days)
- Adjusted start/end times (e.g., 7 AM - 3 PM instead of 9 AM - 5 PM)
- Part-time arrangements

All flexible schedule requests must be approved by your manager and HR. 
Requests should be submitted through the HR Portal.

2.3 CORE HOURS
--------------
Regardless of flexible arrangements, all employees must be available during 
core hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone. This ensures 
overlap for team collaboration and meetings.

2.4 HOME OFFICE STIPEND
-----------------------
Remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time home office setup 
stipend of $500 and a monthly internet/utilities stipend of $75.

To claim: Submit receipts through the expense system within 30 days of purchase.

                   SECTION 3: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
================================================================================

3.1 PAY SCHEDULE
----------------
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. If payday falls on a holiday, payment 
will be processed on the preceding business day.

Direct deposit is required for all employees. Update your banking information 
through the HR Portal under "Payroll Settings."

3.2 SALARY REVIEWS
------------------
Salary reviews occur annually in March, following the performance review cycle. 
Factors considered include:
- Individual performance ratings
- Market compensation data
- Company financial performance
- Internal equity

Off-cycle salary adjustments may occur for promotions, significant role changes, 
or retention purposes.

3.3 HEALTH INSURANCE
--------------------
TechCorp offers comprehensive health insurance through BlueCross BlueShield:

Plans Available:
- PPO Plan: Higher premiums, lower deductibles, wider network
- HDHP Plan: Lower premiums, higher deductibles, HSA eligible

Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.

Employee Contributions (monthly):
- Employee only: $150 (PPO) / $75 (HDHP)
- Employee + Spouse: $350 (PPO) / $175 (HDHP)
- Employee + Children: $300 (PPO) / $150 (HDHP)
- Family: $500 (PPO) / $250 (HDHP)

3.4 DENTAL & VISION
-------------------
Dental Insurance (Delta Dental):
- Preventive care: 100% covered
- Basic procedures: 80% covered
- Major procedures: 50% covered
- Annual maximum: $2,000

Vision Insurance (VSP):
- Annual eye exam: $10 copay
- Frames: $150 allowance every 2 years
- Lenses: $25 copay
- Contact lenses: $150 allowance annually

3.5 RETIREMENT BENEFITS (401K)
------------------------------
TechCorp offers a 401(k) retirement plan with company matching:
- Company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary
- Additional 50% match on contributions between 4-6%
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years

Example: If you earn $100,000 and contribute 6%:
- Your contribution: $6,000
- Company match: $4,000 (100% of first 4%) + $1,000 (50% of next 2%) = $5,000
- Total annual contribution: $11,000

Employees can enroll or change contributions at any time through Fidelity.

3.6 LIFE & DISABILITY INSURANCE
-------------------------------
Life Insurance:
- Basic life: 2x annual salary (company paid)
- Supplemental life: Available for purchase up to 5x salary

Disability Insurance:
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary after 12 weeks (company paid)

3.7 OTHER BENEFITS
------------------
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free confidential counseling
- Gym membership reimbursement: Up to $50/month
- Professional development: $2,000 annual learning budget
- Commuter benefits: Pre-tax transit and parking
- Employee stock purchase plan: 15% discount on company stock
- Tuition reimbursement: Up to $5,250/year for approved programs

                    SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

4.1 PERFORMANCE REVIEW CYCLE
----------------------------
TechCorp conducts formal performance reviews twice per year:
- Mid-year review: July (feedback and development focused)
- Annual review: January (comprehensive evaluation, tied to compensation)

Additionally, managers should provide continuous feedback through regular 
1-on-1 meetings (recommended weekly or bi-weekly).

4.2 PERFORMANCE RATINGS
-----------------------
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:

5 - Exceptional: Consistently exceeds all expectations, role model
4 - Exceeds Expectations: Regularly exceeds goals, strong contributor
3 - Meets Expectations: Consistently meets all job requirements
2 - Needs Improvement: Sometimes falls short, requires development
1 - Unsatisfactory: Consistently fails to meet expectations

Ratings of 2 or below trigger a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

4.3 GOAL SETTING
----------------
All employees must have documented goals using the SMART framework:
- Specific: Clear and well-defined
- Measurable: Quantifiable success criteria
- Achievable: Realistic given resources and constraints
- Relevant: Aligned with team and company objectives
- Time-bound: Clear deadlines

Goals should be set within the first 30 days of each review period and 
documented in the performance management system.

4.4 PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS (PIP)
---------------------------------------
If an employee's performance falls below expectations, a PIP may be initiated:

Duration: Typically 30-90 days depending on the issues identified

PIP Components:
- Specific performance gaps identified
- Clear expectations and success metrics
- Resources and support provided
- Check-in schedule (usually weekly)
- Consequences if improvement is not achieved

Successful completion of a PIP removes the employee from the improvement 
process. Failure to meet PIP requirements may result in termination.

4.5 PROMOTIONS
--------------
Promotions are based on:
- Consistent high performance (rating of 4 or above)
- Demonstration of next-level competencies
- Business need and headcount availability
- Manager recommendation and skip-level approval

To be considered for promotion, discuss your career goals with your manager 
during 1-on-1s and ensure your achievements are documented.

================================================================================
                    SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICS
================================================================================

5.1 PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
------------------------
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Communicate professionally in all interactions
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report concerns through appropriate channels

5.2 ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
--------------------------
TechCorp has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including:
- Sexual harassment
- Discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, or any 
  protected characteristic
- Bullying or intimidation
- Retaliation against those who report concerns

All harassment complaints will be investigated promptly and confidentially. 
Substantiated claims will result in disciplinary action up to and including 
termination.

To report harassment: Contact HR directly, use the anonymous ethics hotline 
(1-800-555-ETHICS), or report through the HR Portal.

5.3 CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
-------------------------
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company 
interests. Examples include:
- Having a financial interest in a competitor or vendor
- Hiring or supervising relatives
- Accepting gifts over $100 from vendors or customers
- Outside employment that interferes with job duties

Disclose any potential conflicts to your manager and HR immediately.

5.4 CONFIDENTIALITY
-------------------
Employees have access to confidential information including:
- Trade secrets and intellectual property
- Customer data and contracts
- Financial information
- Employee personal information

This information must not be shared with unauthorized parties, even after 
employment ends. Violations may result in termination and legal action.

5.5 SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY
-----------------------
When using social media:
- Do not share confidential company information
- Clearly state opinions are your own, not the company's
- Do not disparage colleagues, customers, or competitors
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations

================================================================================
                      SECTION 6: HEALTH & SAFETY
================================================================================

6.1 WORKPLACE SAFETY
--------------------
TechCorp is committed to providing a safe work environment. Employees must:
- Report all injuries, accidents, or near-misses immediately
- Follow all safety protocols and procedures
- Use required protective equipment
- Keep work areas clean and free of hazards
- Know emergency exit locations and procedures

6.2 EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
------------------------
In case of emergency:
- Fire: Evacuate immediately using nearest exit, meet at designated assembly 
  point, do not use elevators
- Medical emergency: Call 911, then notify security at ext. 5555
- Severe weather: Move to interior rooms away from windows
- Active threat: Run, Hide, Fight (in that order)

Emergency contact numbers are posted in all common areas.

6.3 ERGONOMICS
--------------
To prevent repetitive strain injuries:
- Adjust your chair so feet are flat on the floor
- Position monitor at arm's length, top of screen at eye level
- Keep wrists straight when typing
- Take regular breaks (5 minutes every hour)

Request an ergonomic assessment through HR if you experience discomfort.

6.4 MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT
-------------------------
TechCorp recognizes the importance of mental health. Resources available:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 8 free counseling sessions per year
- Mental health days: Use sick leave for mental health needs
- Meditation app subscription (Calm or Headspace)
- Manager training on recognizing and supporting mental health

If you're struggling, please reach out to HR or the EAP. All conversations 
are confidential.

                     SECTION 7: IT & SECURITY POLICIES
================================================================================

7.1 ACCEPTABLE USE
------------------
Company technology resources (computers, phones, internet) are provided for 
business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable if it:
- Does not interfere with job duties
- Does not consume excessive resources
- Complies with all other policies

Prohibited activities include:
- Accessing inappropriate or illegal content
- Installing unauthorized software
- Sharing passwords or login credentials
- Using company resources for personal business ventures

7.2 PASSWORD POLICY
-------------------
All passwords must:
- Be at least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- Be changed every 90 days
- Not be reused for 12 generations
- Not be shared with anyone, including IT

Use the company password manager (LastPass) for secure storage.

7.3 DATA PROTECTION
-------------------
Employees handling personal data must:
- Access only data necessary for job duties
- Store data only in approved systems
- Never transfer data to personal devices or accounts
- Report any data breaches immediately to IT Security

Violations of data protection policies may result in disciplinary action and 
legal consequences.

7.4 BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE (BYOD)
--------------------------------
Personal devices may be used for work with IT approval. Requirements:
- Device must have current security updates
- Company MDM software must be installed
- Device can be remotely wiped if lost or upon termination
- Work data must be kept separate from personal data

Submit a BYOD request through the IT Portal for approval.

7.5 INCIDENT REPORTING
----------------------
Report security incidents immediately:
- Email: security@techcorp.com
- Phone: IT Helpdesk ext. 4357
- After hours: Security hotline 1-800-555-HELP

Incidents include: phishing attempts, malware, unauthorized access, lost/stolen 
devices, and suspicious activity.

                   SECTION 8: TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT
================================================================================

8.1 ONBOARDING
--------------
New employees complete a comprehensive onboarding program:

Week 1:
- Company overview and culture
- HR policies and benefits enrollment
- IT setup and security training
- Meet your team

Weeks 2-4:
- Role-specific training
- Buddy program pairing
- Initial goal setting with manager

90-Day Check-in:
- Formal review of onboarding experience
- Performance expectations discussion
- End of probationary period evaluation

8.2 REQUIRED TRAINING
---------------------
All employees must complete annually:
- Security awareness training (2 hours)
- Anti-harassment training (1 hour)
- Data privacy training (1 hour)
- Ethics and compliance training (1 hour)

Managers must additionally complete:
- Leadership fundamentals (4 hours)
- Performance management training (2 hours)
- Inclusive leadership (2 hours)

8.3 PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT BUDGET
-----------------------------------
Each employee receives $2,000 annually for professional development:

Eligible expenses:
- Conferences and seminars
- Online courses and certifications
- Books and learning materials
- Professional membership fees

To use: Submit a development request through the HR Portal for manager approval 
before incurring expenses.

8.4 TUITION REIMBURSEMENT
-------------------------
Employees pursuing degrees related to their role may receive up to $5,250/year:

Eligibility:
- Employed for at least 1 year
- Course is job-related or part of approved degree program
- Grade of B or higher (or pass for pass/fail courses)

Process:
1. Submit tuition reimbursement application before starting course
2. Receive pre-approval from HR and manager
3. Complete course and submit transcript
4. Receive reimbursement within 30 days

Note: Employees must remain employed for 1 year after receiving reimbursement 
or repay the amount on a prorated basis.

8.5 INTERNAL MOBILITY
---------------------
TechCorp encourages internal career growth. To apply for internal positions:
- Meet minimum qualifications listed
- Be in current role for at least 12 months
- Have satisfactory performance (rating 3 or above)
- Discuss with current manager before applying

Internal candidates receive priority consideration for open positions.

               SECTION 9: GRIEVANCE & COMPLAINT PROCEDURES
================================================================================

9.1 OPEN DOOR POLICY
--------------------
TechCorp maintains an open door policy. Employees are encouraged to discuss 
concerns with their manager first. If uncomfortable doing so, employees may 
contact:
- Skip-level manager
- Human Resources
- Anonymous ethics hotline

9.2 FORMAL GRIEVANCE PROCESS
----------------------------
For formal complaints:

Step 1: Submit written complaint to HR within 30 days of incident
Step 2: HR acknowledges receipt within 2 business days
Step 3: HR investigates (typically 10-15 business days)
Step 4: HR communicates findings and resolution
Step 5: Appeal to VP of HR if unsatisfied (within 5 business days)

All grievances are handled confidentially to the extent possible.

9.3 NON-RETALIATION
-------------------
Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly 
prohibited. Retaliation includes:
- Termination or demotion
- Reduction in pay or hours
- Negative performance reviews as punishment
- Harassment or exclusion

Report any suspected retaliation to HR immediately.

9.4 WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION
----------------------------
Employees who report illegal activity or serious policy violations are 
protected under company policy and applicable laws. Reports can be made to:
- Direct supervisor
- Human Resources
- Legal department
- Anonymous ethics hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS

================================================================================
                  SECTION 10: TERMINATION & OFFBOARDING
================================================================================

10.1 VOLUNTARY RESIGNATION
--------------------------
Employees wishing to resign should:
- Provide written notice to manager (2 weeks minimum, 4 weeks for managers)
- Complete exit interview with HR
- Return all company property on last day
- Ensure knowledge transfer to colleagues

Failure to provide adequate notice may affect rehire eligibility and final 
pay timing.

10.2 INVOLUNTARY TERMINATION
----------------------------
Employment may be terminated for:
- Performance issues (after PIP process)
- Policy violations
- Misconduct
- Position elimination
- Business restructuring

Employees will receive:
- Explanation of termination reason
- Final paycheck including accrued PTO
- COBRA information for benefits continuation
- Severance (if applicable, based on tenure and circumstances)

10.3 OFFBOARDING CHECKLIST
--------------------------
On your last day:
□ Return laptop, phone, and all equipment
□ Return ID badge and parking pass
□ Remove personal belongings from office/desk
□ Transfer files and knowledge to designated colleague
□ Complete exit interview with HR
□ Update personal contact information for tax documents

10.4 REFERENCES
---------------
TechCorp provides employment verification only (dates of employment, title). 
Personal references from colleagues are at their individual discretion.

10.5 REHIRE POLICY
------------------
Former employees may be eligible for rehire if:
- Left in good standing
- Provided adequate notice
- No policy violations on record
- Position is available

Apply through the standard hiring process. Previous tenure may be considered 
for benefits purposes.
